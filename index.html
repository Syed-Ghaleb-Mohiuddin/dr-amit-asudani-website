About Dr. Amit

Orthodontist | Breathwork Coach | Storyteller

Dr. Amit Asudani is an orthodontist and breathwork specialist who began his journey in Dubai in 2010. With a Master's in Orthodontics and Dento-Facial Orthopedics, he has spent over a decade refining smiles through digital orthodontics, aesthetic precision, and mindful care. His approach bridges modern dental innovation with the art of conscious breathing, guiding patients toward both functional and aesthetic balance.

After years of clinical experience in Dubai and Amsterdam, Dr. Amit founded his practice at Oval Dental Clinic — where dentistry meets awareness. His philosophy blends aligner-based orthodontics, breathing pattern correction, and holistic performance training, helping patients achieve more than just a smile: a healthier, more aligned life.
